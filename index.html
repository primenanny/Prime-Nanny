The Process

How a placement works

From your first message to your new nanny starting work, here's what to expect at each stage.

  • 1

    You tell us what you need

    We start with a quick intake call or WhatsApp chat to understand your household, the role, hours, your children's ages, live-in or live-out, any special requirements. The more we know, the better the match.

  • 2

    We search and shortlist

    We match from our existing pool of verified candidates or actively recruit if needed. We only consider people who fit your specific situation, not just anyone available.

  • 3

    Every candidate is properly checked

    Before we present anyone to you, they've been through a SAPS criminal check, ID or passport verification, reference calls with previous employers, and a skills interview with us. No shortcuts.

  • 4

    You choose from a shortlist of 2 to 3

    We send you a small shortlist of candidates who match your brief. You decide who to meet. You decide who to hire. We don't push anyone on you.

  • 5

    Salary is agreed directly between you and the worker

    We don't set salaries and we don't add a margin on top. You and the worker agree what works for both of you. Our only charge is the once-off placement fee.

  • 6

    Placement confirmed, you're covered

    Once the placement is confirmed, you pay the once-off fee and your new nanny or housekeeper starts. If anything goes wrong in the first 30 days, we find a replacement for free.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch directly.

WhatsApp us or email primenanny@gmail.com

How much does it cost?
You pay a once-off placement fee when the placement is confirmed, typically R2,500 to R6,000 for standard roles, up to R15,000 for premium placements. There are no monthly fees and no ongoing charges. The worker's salary is agreed directly between you and them.
Am I the employer or is Prime Nanny?
You are the employer. Prime Nanny is a placement agency, we find and verify the worker, but the employment relationship is entirely between you and the worker. We put this in writing for both parties so there's no confusion.
How long does a placement take?
Most placements are completed within one to two weeks of the initial intake call. Urgent or short-term placements can often be done faster. Premium placements, household managers and specialised carers, may take a little longer.
What exactly does the 30-day guarantee cover?
If a placement doesn't work out within the first 30 days for any reason, we find you a replacement candidate at no additional charge. We keep a reserve pool of pre-screened candidates available for this reason.
Is there a fee for workers to register?
No, registration is always free for candidates. We will never charge a worker to be placed. Our fee is paid by the household, not the worker. If anyone tells you Prime Nanny charges workers, that's not us.
How is my personal information handled?
Prime Nanny is POPIA compliant. We collect only what's needed for the placement process, store it securely, and never share it with third parties without your consent. Candidates sign a consent form before any background check is run.
